Support

Zwift Coureur for iPhone and iPad

Zwift Coureur ranks frame and wheelset combinations by estimated finish time for a route, calculated for your own weight, height and FTP.

Need help, found a mistake, or want to suggest something?
Email support@decoureur.eu. Please mention your device and iOS version, and the route or equipment involved.

Do I need a Zwift account?

No. The app does not connect to Zwift and works entirely on its own.

Does it cost anything?

No. There is no subscription, no in-app purchase and no advertising.

Where do the times come from?

The app simulates the elevation profile of the route using standard cycling physics, with your weight, height and FTP. The result is an estimate, not a promise: it tells you which setup is faster and by roughly how much. The figures behind it come from published bot tests; where a frame or wheelset has never been tested, they are estimated from what is known about it.

Why does the app ask for Apple Health?

Only to fill in your weight and height. You can refuse and type them in yourself; nothing else changes. See the privacy policy.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. All data is included in the app. A connection is only used to add material published after the app was released.

Why is my equipment not in the list?

Add it in the Garage tab, then use the "My garage" filter on a route. If a frame or wheelset is missing from the Garage search entirely, email us and it will be added in the next data update.

Zwift Coureur is an independent app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Zwift, Inc.
